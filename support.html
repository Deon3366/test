Ways you can help and support us.

Adoption isn't the only way you can support our rescue. If you would like to help us but are not in a position to adopt, why not consider one of these alternatives.

Fostering

Fostering is the vital stepping stone in our work. It gives our cats the chance to feel safe during, what can be a very stressful time for them.

Cats, as we know, are sensitive creatures and not all settle in the rescue. So having foster homes that they can stay in until they find their forever homes is so important.

If you would like to help us by fostering, please contact us. We would love to talk to you.

Fundraising

As a not for profit organisation we rely on fundraising and donations. If you fancy holding a fundraising event for us, we would be overjoyed!

Here a few simple ideas for fundraising:

  • Cake sale
  • Car boot sale
  • Quiz night
  • Coffee morning

Donations

It costs, on average, £200 to rehome a cat. Obviously if they need medical attention it can be considerably more. Donations help us to keep helping the cats that need us.

We would be extremely grateful if you could consider making a one off donation or a standing order to help us on a monthly basis.
