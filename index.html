Get to Know Us

At Happy Paws Cat Rescue, our story began with a simple mission — to give every cat a second chance at life. What started as a small effort has grown into a dedicated rescue working tirelessly to save, shelter, and rehome homeless and neglected cats.

Driven by compassion and community support, we ensure each feline receives the love, care, and medical attention they deserve. Whether it's a kitten in need of a warm place or an older cat looking for a forever home, we're here to help.
