About Happy Paws Cat Rescue

Founded by Katrina, Happy Paws Cat Rescue started with a simple passion: giving every cat a second chance at life. What began in Stockton in the North East of England has grown to reach branches across Birmingham, Banbury, and nationwide.

Our network of dedicated volunteers and foster carers work tirelessly to rescue strays, rehabilitate sick or injured cats, and find them loving forever homes.
