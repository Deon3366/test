ABOUT US

Happy Paws Cat Rescue was founded by myself, Katrina. I've had a passion for helping cats all my life—ever since I was a little girl, I would always try to help cats in need. I always dreamed of opening my own rescue when I got older, and now that dream is a reality.

The rescue began three years ago in the North East of England, in a small town called Stockton. From there, we expanded further, and we now also have a branch in Birmingham, allowing us to rescue and rehome cats nationwide. We're even taking over Banbury, having rehomed so many of our rescues there and connecting with some lovely fosters in the area too.

Our team is made up of dedicated volunteers who work tirelessly to ensure that every cat in our care receives the love and support they deserve. From rescuing strays to rehabilitating and rehoming them, we're committed to making a difference in the lives of cats in need.
